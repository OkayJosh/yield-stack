# The Yield Stack

> *Positioning Strategically for Macro Trends Affecting Fintech Products*

**The Yield Stack** is an open-source macro-fintech research knowledge base, editorial archive, and architectural diagram repository focused on **capital plumbing, sovereign settlement rails, project finance, downstream trade finance, and programmable treasury infrastructure** across Nigeria, Sub-Saharan Africa (AfCFTA), and emerging South-South / Eurasian corridors.

- **LinkedIn Newsletter:** [The Yield Stack on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/newsletters/yield-stack-7494346716641505280/)
- **Reference Implementation (Programmable Treasury Engine):** [`OkayJosh/corporate-treasury`](https://github.com/OkayJosh/corporate-treasury) (Go, PostgreSQL, Redis)

---

## Core Thesis Pillars

1. **Sovereign Rails & Cross-Border Settlement:** Moving past USD correspondent-banking surcharges and trapped local currencies via PAPSS, BRICS Pay, NDB local-currency financing, and dual-regime compliance gateways.
2. **Merchant Banking vs. Investment Banking vs. Fintech Orchestration:** Mapping where SEC investment banks, CBN merchant banks (16% CRR, ₦100M floor), and B2B fintech commercial sponsor nodes each win.
3. **Downstream Trade Finance & Capital Velocity:** Analyzing how leveraged working-capital instruments (BGs, DFFs, LPO/IDF, SBLCs, Back-to-Back LCs, CMA stock finance) turn thin per-unit spreads into high annualized ROE.
4. **Programmable Verification & Treasury Control:** Replacing manual paper audits and fragile CFO spreadsheets with hardware delivery oracles, ring-fenced FBO virtual NUBANs, priority repayment waterfalls, and enforceable mathematical invariants.
5. **Agentic Payments:** Architecting machine-to-machine settlement plumbing (`x402`, Web Bot Auth, ACP, UCP) for autonomous software agents.
