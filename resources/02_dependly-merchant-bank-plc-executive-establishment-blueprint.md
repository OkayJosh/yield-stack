## **1\. Executive Summary & Strategic Rationale**

Dependly has established significant market traction as a next-generation "verifiable trust layer" facilitating automated escrow, condition-based contract execution, and milestone-linked payment settlement. While operating as a pure-play technology platform offers agile execution, it inherently constrains growth due to structural dependencies on third-party commercial bank partners, payment processor clearing friction, counterparty deposit risks, and balance-sheet limitations.

The establishment of **Dependly Merchant Bank Plc** represents a strategic transformation from a software-as-a-service (SaaS) intermediary into an institutional wholesale financial engine. By securing a National Merchant Banking License from the Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN), Dependly will embed its proprietary verification infrastructure directly on top of a fully licensed banking balance sheet, combining systemic institutional trust with programmable financial engineering.

## **3\. Statutory Licensing Process & Regulatory Roadmap**

Under the Central Bank of Nigeria regulations and BOFIA 2020, obtaining a Merchant Banking License involves a three-phase procedure executed with rigorous governance and anti-money laundering compliance:

### **Phase 1: Application for Approval-in-Principle (AIP)**

* **Formal Petition:** Formal application addressed to the Governor of the Central Bank of Nigeria accompanied by a non-refundable application fee of ₦500,000.  
* **Capital Escrow Deposit:** Mandatory placement of the full ₦50,000,000,000 minimum share capital into a designated CBN Escrow Account. These funds remain securely held in escrow and are 100% refundable if the application is declined. While held in escrow, the CBN typically invests the capital in short-term government securities (Treasury Bills).  
* **Detailed Business Case:** Submission of a 5-year Comprehensive Feasibility Report, audited statements of promoters, draft Memorandum and Articles of Association (MEMART), and corporate governance charters.  
* **Shareholder & UBO Screening:** Comprehensive background checks, anti-money laundering/counter-terrorist financing (AML/CFT) vetting, and verification of Ultimate Beneficial Owners (UBO) for all equity holders with stakes exceeding 5%.

### **Phase 2: Grant of Final License**

* Within six (6) months of AIP approval, the promoters must submit evidence of corporate incorporation with the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC).  
* Payment of the statutory licensing fee of ₦5,000,000 to the CBN.  
* Execution of long-term commercial lease or title for a secure, institutional-grade Head Office.  
* Submission of letters of appointment and CVs of cleared executive management and board candidates satisfying the CBN "Fit and Proper Persons" regime.

### **Phase 3: Pre-Commencement Checks & Clearing House Admission**

* Onsite inspection of physical security, Disaster Recovery (DR) data facilities, and enterprise core banking software.  
* Formal integration into the Nigeria Inter-Bank Settlement System (NIBSS), the Central Bank RTGS, and the Clearing House.  
* Minutes of the inaugural pre-commencement Board of Directors meeting confirming formal capital allocation and risk limits.
