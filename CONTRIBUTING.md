# Contributing to The Yield Stack

Thanks for your interest in contributing to **The Yield Stack**!

## Ways to Contribute
- **Fact-Checks & Market Data Updates:** Central bank circulars (CBN MPR/CRR), gantry benchmarks, FX/trade-finance rates, or regulatory updates (NMDPRA, SEC, AfCFTA, PAPSS).
- **Architecture Diagrams:** Improvements or additions to `.drawio` and Mermaid diagrams in `articles/diagrams/`.
- **Topic Proposals:** Open a GitHub Issue proposing a macro-fintech plumbing topic, counter-thesis, or real-world settlement case study.

## Pull Request Guidelines
1. Keep prose clear, analytical, and free of em dashes.
2. Cite primary regulatory, central-bank, or market data sources for quantitative claims.
3. Verify all unit-economics math before opening a PR.
